A little care for your privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Publisher: BigT All IT

Contact: bigtallit.dev@gmail.com

Brick Sprout is a garden puzzle game published by BigT All IT. This policy explains the information stored by the game and the information processed by optional advertising, Google Play Games and sharing features.

Your garden stays on your device

Journey and Endless play do not require a Brick Sprout account. We do not operate a game-account or cloud-save service for your puzzle layouts.

The game stores progress and settings on your device: completed and selected plots, bed positions and move history, discovered plants, biome rewards, Sunbeams, supplies, board finishes, cached hints, sound and accessibility choices, and reward receipts. Endless separately stores the current board, offered shapes, score, personal best, harvested lines, streak and random-generation state.

These local saves are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. The Android game bundles its art, sound and puzzles. Android backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes these local saves; they are not automatically synchronized by the leaderboard feature.

Age choices and optional ads

Brick Sprout is intended for people aged 13 and older. Every game mode can be played without watching ads. When an age choice is needed for optional ad rewards, Android asks whether you are 13–15, 16 or older, or prefer not to say. Only this coarse choice is stored locally; the game does not ask for a date of birth or exact age.

For a 13–15 or undisclosed choice, the application does not request a Google consent update, explicitly initialize Mobile Ads, or load or show rewarded ads. A 16+ choice enables the consent-check path. You can change the saved choice through Privacy choices.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to update consent status and show a required privacy message. For a stored 16+ choice, this status can refresh when the app opens. The application's explicit Mobile Ads initialization and initial ad loading require an optional reward request and an allowed canRequestAds result. Loading and display recheck that permission. After initialization, the SDK may preload a later rewarded ad while ads remain allowed.

Google's standard Mobile Ads startup provider remains included and can run when the Android process starts. The application's request guards do not mean that all SDK startup processing is absent. Privacy choices also opens Google's privacy-options form when available or required. Google stores its own consent and regional privacy signals.

Completing an optional rewarded ad can grant the reward displayed in the game. Starting, cancelling or being unable to load an ad does not itself grant a reward. Puzzles, Undo, retries and normal progression remain available without rewarded ads.

Information processed by Google advertising

Google Mobile Ads may process IP-derived approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers to deliver and measure ads, maintain the service and prevent abuse. Test ads and nonpersonalized ad requests do not mean that no information is processed.

The Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions are removed from this release. This does not remove every SDK identifier or other data flow, including on older Android versions. See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Optional Google Play Games leaderboards

When leaderboard support is configured for a release, you can choose Leaderboard to connect to Google Play Games. If it is not configured, the action is unavailable. On first use, a confirmation offers Connect & view or Keep playing offline. This choice is stored locally.

Google's Play Games SDK initializes when the Android app starts so its sign-in lifecycle works correctly. Google may automatically authenticate an existing Play Games profile according to your Google settings. Automatic creation of a new game profile is suppressed; the game requests interactive sign-in or profile creation when you choose to connect through the leaderboard.

Sharing your Endless personal-best score requires the locally remembered Connect & view choice. The game keeps a validated best score on your device, including a pending score for retry after a restart or failed connection. Opening the leaderboard authenticates if needed and attempts to upload that best before displaying Google's leaderboard. After you have opted in, later finalized runs may upload an improved best when Google reports that you are authenticated. Puzzles remain playable offline without choosing to share a score.

Your gamer identity, avatar, score and rank may be visible in Google's leaderboard interface according to your Google profile settings. Brick Sprout does not calculate or invent a global rank. The game does not directly fetch or store your player ID, display name or avatar in its own save, and does not request an email address, contacts, Google password, friend list or cloud-saved puzzle layout through this feature.

Google's SDK processes authentication, profile, service analytics and diagnostic information. See the Play Games SDK data disclosure, authentication information, and Play Games profile and privacy controls. You can manage Google-hosted profile and game data through Google's settings. Uninstalling Brick Sprout does not by itself delete Google-hosted records.

Sharing a garden or score

Share prepares a short text summary of a completed Journey plot, biome or Endless run. Android opens its Sharesheet with plain text. No screenshot or game-save file is attached. The game does not read your contacts, choose a recipient or send a message automatically. You choose the receiving app and decide whether to send; that app's privacy rules apply.

In a supported browser, Share opens the browser share sheet. If that is unavailable or unsuccessful, the game may copy the prepared text or show a manual-copy dialog. Cancelling the browser share sheet does not silently copy the text.

Shared text contains the relevant result or personal best and a public game link only when one is configured. It excludes private development URLs and does not add device or private player identifiers. The game does not store a recipient list.

Moving your garden with a backup

The optional Move your garden feature creates selectable plain-text backup data containing your local Journey and Endless progress, supplies, settings and game reward receipts. It does not include the device’s ad-age choice, Google consent settings or Play Games sign-in choice.

You choose whether to copy the backup to the clipboard or save it somewhere you can access on another device or app version. The game does not automatically upload backups to a server. Your device’s clipboard or a storage, messaging or sharing service you choose may handle that text under its own privacy rules. Keep backup text private and share it only where you intend.

Import checks the pasted backup and asks for confirmation before replacing this app’s saved Journey and Endless gardens. Gardens cannot be merged. Import does not change your device’s ad privacy or sign-in choices.

Support, retention and your choices

For privacy questions or requests, contact BigT All IT at bigtallit.dev@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider sends the address and message you choose to include. Please send only the information needed for your request. We use support correspondence to respond to and handle that request.

Local game data remains until it is cleared or removed with the app. Google-hosted advertising, consent and Play Games information follows the relevant provider's controls and retention practices. We do not control the retention policies of receiving share apps or Google services.

You can play without using optional sharing, advertising or leaderboards; change the available privacy choices; manage your Google profile; and clear local app storage. Clearing local storage removes progress and supplies on that device. For provider-held data, use the provider's privacy controls or contact us for help identifying the relevant service.

Visiting this public policy page

This public policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages hosting disclosure.

We do not add analytics, advertising or tracking scripts to this policy page. Visiting it does not read or upload your local Brick Sprout game save.

Security and policy changes

The Android app disables cleartext traffic, mixed WebView content, and WebView file and content access. Google states that Mobile Ads and Play Games SDK information is encrypted in transit. These measures do not guarantee that every device or service is free from risk.

We may update this policy when features, providers or practices change. The effective date at the top identifies this version. Contact BigT All IT at bigtallit.dev@gmail.com with questions about this policy.

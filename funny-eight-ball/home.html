Funny Eight Ball

Mystical fortunes. Dry wit. Questionable cosmic confidence.

Think of a question and shake your phone. A cryptic, sarcastic answer appears in the ball, with a prerecorded Sage voice reading.

Make the oracle your own

  • 1,024 humorous fortunes with bundled AI-generated Sage speech.
  • 21 visual styles, including the original Cosmic design.
  • Independent ball, background and visual-effect choices.
  • Recent fortunes, favorites and image sharing through Android.
  • Native shake detection, TalkBack controls and motion preferences.

Fortunes and recorded voice work offline. The app uses the phone's accelerometer to detect shaking and does not record your question. It is intended for people aged 16 and older. Eligible players may see a bottom banner after age and advertising privacy checks. Every game feature remains available without advertising or an age disclosure.

Funny Eight Ball is for entertainment. Store testing and availability are managed through Google Play; this page does not provide an installation or tester opt-in link.

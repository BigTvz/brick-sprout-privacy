Funny Eight Ball

Mystical fortunes. Dry wit. Questionable cosmic confidence.

Think of a question and gently shake your phone for one of 1,024 funny fortunes with prerecorded Sage speech. The original shake-only oracle remains alongside 21 visual sets with independent ball, background and effect choices.

Your Daily Dose of Sage

Open a separate dated card with no eight-ball. The daily collection includes 500 advice entries and 30 rotating replies when you ask for another piece. Reopening, replaying and sharing keep today's same advice.

An optional local reminder uses your selected approximate time. Android can delay delivery to save battery. It opens the daily screen and never starts spoken audio in the background. Reminders stay off without the required permission.

All 1,554 AI-generated prerecorded Sage clips, fortunes, advice and designs work offline. The app uses the accelerometer locally and does not record your question. Daily sharing creates an image of the displayed advice and date.

The game is intended for people aged 16 and older. Eligible players may see a bottom banner after age and advertising privacy checks. Every game feature remains available without advertising or an age disclosure.

Google Play testing and availability have separate review steps. This page provides no unverified installation or tester opt-in link.

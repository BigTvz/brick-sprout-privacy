Funny Eight Ball privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Publisher: BigT All IT

Privacy and support contact: bigtallit.dev@gmail.com

Funny Eight Ball is an Android entertainment game. Shake your phone to reveal a humorous fortune, hear its prerecorded voice, and choose the ball, background and visual effects. This policy explains local game data, advertising and sharing.

Fortunes and settings on your device

You do not need an account. BigT All IT does not operate a Funny Eight Ball account, cloud-save service or developer analytics server.

The game stores your recent fortunes, favorites, fortune counter, last answer, visual selections, motion preferences, sound, voice and vibration settings, and coarse advertising age choice in private app storage. These game saves are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Android app backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local data.

All 1,024 fortunes and their Sage recordings are bundled with the app. The voice recordings were generated using AI before release. Playback does not send a question or live speech to OpenAI. The app does not record your voice or request microphone access. It uses the accelerometer on the device to detect shaking; it does not upload motion readings. Fortunes and recorded voice work offline.

Age choices and banner advertising

Funny Eight Ball is intended for people aged 16 and older. A local age choice offers 16 or older, Under 16, and Prefer not to say. Dismissing the choice records an undisclosed age. Only the coarse choice is saved on your device; the app does not request an exact age or date of birth.

For an Under 16 or undisclosed choice, application code does not request a Google consent update, explicitly initialize Mobile Ads or request a banner. Every game feature remains available without advertising or an age disclosure. You can change the choice through Settings, then Ad privacy.

For the 16+ path, Google's User Messaging Platform refreshes consent information when the app launches and presents a privacy message where required. The app checks Google's allowed-ad-request result before explicitly initializing and loading its bottom banner. Ad privacy also opens Google's privacy-options form when required. Google manages its own consent and regional privacy signals. Depending on the region and choices, declining personalized advertising may still permit limited ads.

This release uses nonpersonalized ad requests, restricts ad content to Google's G rating, and disables publisher first-party ID. Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions are removed. Those settings do not eliminate every advertising identifier or technical data flow. The app's request guards are not a claim that all third-party technical processing is absent.

Information processed by Google advertising

Google Mobile Ads can process general location inferred from IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and device or other identifiers. It uses this information for ad delivery and measurement, service analytics and abuse prevention. Nonpersonalized or test ads can still involve data processing. Google documents encryption in transit for Mobile Ads information.

See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. Google-held advertising and consent information follows Google's controls and retention practices.

Sharing a fortune

Share creates an image of the selected fortune and opens Android's Sharesheet. You choose the receiving app and decide whether to send it. The game grants that app temporary access to the prepared image. It does not read your contacts, choose a recipient, send a message automatically or upload the image to a developer server. The receiving app's privacy practices apply.

Support, retention and your choices

For privacy questions, contact BigT All IT at bigtallit.dev@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider sends your address and the message you choose to include. We use that correspondence to handle your request. Please include only the information needed for support.

Local saves remain until you clear app storage or remove the app. You can change the available age and advertising choices, play offline and choose whether to share a fortune. Clearing app storage also removes your favorites and settings. Uninstalling or contacting support does not automatically delete information held by Google or a receiving app. Use the relevant provider's privacy controls; we can help identify the service involved. We do not promise a deletion period for data controlled by another provider.

This policy website

The published policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no added analytics, advertising scripts or external fonts. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Security and updates

The Android app disables cleartext network traffic. These measures do not guarantee that every device or service is free from risk. We may update this policy when features, providers or practices change. The effective date identifies this version.

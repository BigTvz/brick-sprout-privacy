Funny Eight Ball privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Publisher: BigT All IT

Privacy and support contact: bigtallit.dev@gmail.com

Funny Eight Ball is an Android entertainment app with humorous shake fortunes and a separate daily advice card called Your Daily Dose of Sage. This policy explains local app data, recordings, reminders, advertising and sharing.

Fortunes, daily advice and settings on your device

You do not need an account. BigT All IT does not operate a Funny Eight Ball account, cloud-save service or developer analytics server.

The app stores your recent fortunes, favorites, fortune counter, last answer, visual selections, motion preferences, sound, voice and vibration settings, and coarse advertising age choice in private app storage. Daily advice additionally stores a local selection seed, dated readings/read state, extra-request rotation and reminder choices. These local saves are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. Android app backup is disabled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local data.

The app bundles 1,024 funny fortunes, 500 daily advice entries and 30 rotating replies to extra daily requests, with 1,554 prerecorded Sage clips. The recordings were generated using AI before release. Playback does not send your question or live speech to OpenAI. The app does not record your voice or request microphone access. The accelerometer detects shaking locally; motion readings are not uploaded. Fortunes, daily advice, recordings and designs work offline.

Your daily reading uses the device's local calendar date and a selection saved on that device. Reopening, replaying or sharing the current card keeps that day's advice. Asking for another piece selects a rotating reply rather than replacing today's advice. Device date or timezone changes can affect the calendar date; there is no online time verification or claim of tamper resistance.

Optional local reminders

Daily reminders are off until you choose a reminder time and allow notifications where Android requires permission. The time is approximate; Android may delay the reminder to save battery. The app schedules reminders locally, aims for at most one per local day and skips a reminder if that day's advice has already been read.

A reminder opens Your Daily Dose of Sage. It does not play recorded speech in the background. The app can restore its local schedule after a restart, app update or time/timezone change. No push-notification server, exact-alarm permission or reminder account is used. Reminder time, permission state and local read/notification state are not uploaded to a developer server. You can turn reminders off in the app or Android notification settings; denying permission leaves reminders off and the advice remains available.

Age choices and banner advertising

Funny Eight Ball is intended for people aged 16 and older. A local age choice offers 16 or older, Under 16, and Prefer not to say. Dismissing the choice records an undisclosed age. Only the coarse choice is saved; the app does not request an exact age or date of birth.

For an Under 16 or undisclosed choice, application code does not request a Google consent update, explicitly initialize Mobile Ads or request a banner. Every app feature remains available without advertising or an age disclosure. Settings, then Ad privacy, changes the choice.

For the 16+ path, Google's User Messaging Platform refreshes consent information at launch and presents a privacy message where required. The app checks Google's allowed-ad-request result before explicitly initializing and loading its bottom banner. Ad privacy also opens Google's privacy-options form when required. Google manages its consent and regional privacy signals. Declining personalized advertising may still permit limited ads, depending on the region and choices.

This release uses nonpersonalized requests, restricts ad content to Google's G rating and disables publisher first-party ID. Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions are removed. These settings do not eliminate every advertising identifier or technical data flow. Application request guards are not a claim that all third-party technical processing is absent.

Information processed by Google advertising

Google Mobile Ads can process general location inferred from IP address, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device or other identifiers for ad delivery and measurement, analytics and abuse prevention. Nonpersonalized or test ads can still involve processing. Google documents encryption in transit for Mobile Ads information.

See Google's privacy policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure. Google-held advertising and consent information follows Google's controls and retention practices.

Sharing a fortune or daily card

Share creates an image and opens Android's Sharesheet. Eight-ball sharing shows the selected fortune in its ball; daily sharing shows the exact displayed advice, its date and Your Daily Dose of Sage, without a ball. You choose the receiving app and decide whether to send it. The app grants that app temporary access to the prepared image. It does not read contacts, choose recipients, send a message automatically or upload the image to a developer server. The receiving app's privacy practices apply.

Support, retention and your choices

For privacy questions, contact BigT All IT at bigtallit.dev@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider sends your address and the message you include. We use that correspondence to handle your request. Please include only the information needed for support.

Local saves remain until you clear app storage or remove the app. You can change the available age/advertising choices, use the app offline, disable reminders and choose whether to share. Clearing app storage removes favorites, daily state and settings. Uninstalling or contacting support does not automatically delete information held by Google or a receiving app. Use the relevant provider's privacy controls; we can help identify the service. We do not promise a deletion period for another provider's data.

Security and updates

The Android app disables cleartext network traffic. These measures do not guarantee that every device or service is free from risk. We may update this policy when features, providers or practices change. The effective date identifies this version.

A little puzzle. A world in bloom.

Garden journey

Drag each bed into place and plan around established obstacles. Explore changing biomes, larger gardens and new planting rules. Discover flowers, vegetables and other plant varieties in your botanical journal.

Endless Garden

Choose from three ready-facing shapes and fill rows or columns to harvest. Keep room for the next beds, build bloom streaks and grow your personal best.

Core gameplay works offline, at your own pace. Journey includes free Undo and retries. Optional rewarded ads can provide extra supplies; normal play does not require watching ads. Garden progress stays on your device, with an optional text backup to move it.
